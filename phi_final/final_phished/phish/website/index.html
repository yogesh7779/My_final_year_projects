      Stay Safe Online

      Essential tips to protect yourself from phishing attacks

      1

      Check the URL

      Always verify the domain name. Phishing sites often use misspellings or extra characters.

      2

      Look for HTTPS

      Legitimate sites usually use secure connections. Check for the padlock icon in your browser.

      3

      Be Suspicious

      If an offer seems too good to be true or creates urgency, it's likely a scam.
